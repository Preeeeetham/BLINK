## 1. Metric Classification Taxonomy

Every quantity reported by Project BLINK is categorized into one of six distinct scientific tiers:

| Tier Code | Classification Tier | Definition | Can Display % Probability? | Ground Truth Needed? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **[OBS]** | **Observed** | Direct calibrated sensor measurement from INSAT-3DS. | No | No (Is GT) |
| **[DER]** | **Derived** | Deterministic mathematical calculation on observations. | No | No |
| **[MOD]** | **Model Output** | Synthesized/forecasted field generated by BLINK. | No | No |
| **[HEU]** | **Heuristic Indicator** | Rule-based index combining physical features without calibration. | **NO** | No |
| **[PRB]** | **Calibrated Probability** | Statistical likelihood backed by event-level empirical ground truth. | **Yes (Only when validated)** | Required |
| **[VAL]** | **Validation Metric** | Comparison between model output and an independent observation. | No | **Strictly Required** |

## 2. Complete Metric Provenance Table

### Metric 1: Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (`psnr_db`)
- **Classification:** `[VAL]` Validation Metric
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{PSNR} = 10 \cdot \log_{10}\left(\frac{\text{MAX}_I^2}{\text{MSE}}\right) \quad \text{where} \quad \text{MSE} = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum_{(x, y) \in \Omega} (\hat{I}(x, y) - I_{\text{gt}}(x, y))^2$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:**
  - Prediction: $\hat{I} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times C \times H \times W}$
  - Ground Truth: $I_{\text{gt}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times C \times H \times W}$
- **Units:** Decibels ($\text{dB}$)
- **Dynamic Range ($\text{MAX}_I$):**
  - Normalized domain: $\text{MAX}_I = 1.0$
  - Physical domain ($T_{\text{B}}$): $\text{MAX}_I = 140.0\text{ K}$
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** **MANDATORY INDEPENDENT OBSERVATION**. Must be a real held-out observation frame recorded at the query timestep. Comparing prediction against linear blend $(1-\tau)I_0 + \tau I_1$ is **strictly forbidden**.
- **Default Fallback When GT Absent:** Reports `None` (UI displays `N/A` with subtitle `No independent temporal ground truth`).
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_psnr`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Horé & Ziou (2010), *Image Quality Metrics: PSNR vs. SSIM*, ICPR; Vandal et al. (2021), IEEE TGRS.
- **Limitations:** PSNR penalizes pixel-level spatial shifts heavily even when meteorological cloud features are visually and dynamically plausible (double penalty effect).

### Metric 2: Structural Similarity Index (`ssim`)
- **Classification:** `[VAL]` Validation Metric
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{SSIM} = \frac{(2\mu_{\hat{I}}\mu_{I_{\text{gt}}} + C_1)(2\sigma_{\hat{I} I_{\text{gt}}} + C_2)}{(\mu_{\hat{I}}^2 + \mu_{I_{\text{gt}}}^2 + C_1)(\sigma_{\hat{I}}^2 + \sigma_{I_{\text{gt}}}^2 + C_2)}$$
  with $C_1 = (0.01 \cdot \text{MAX}_I)^2, C_2 = (0.03 \cdot \text{MAX}_I)^2$.
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $\hat{I}, I_{\text{gt}} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times C \times H \times W}$, Gaussian kernel $\sigma = 1.5$, window size 11.
- **Units:** Dimensionless, bounded in $[-1.0, 1.0]$ (typically $[0.80, 1.0]$ for satellite clouds).
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** **MANDATORY INDEPENDENT OBSERVATION**. If absent, returns `None` (`N/A`).
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_ssim`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Wang, Bovik, Sheikh, Simoncelli (2004), *Image Quality Assessment: From Error Visibility to Structural Similarity*, IEEE TIP.
- **Limitations:** Evaluates structural pattern coherence over local patches; may overlook absolute radiative energy offsets if local contrast is preserved.

### Metric 3: Brightness Temperature Root Mean Square Error (`rmse_k`)
- **Classification:** `[VAL]` Validation Metric
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{RMSE}_{\text{BT}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum_{(x, y) \in \Omega} (\hat{T}_{\text{B}}(x, y) - T_{\text{B, gt}}(x, y))^2}$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $\hat{T}_{\text{B}}, T_{\text{B, gt}} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$ in Kelvin.
- **Units:** Kelvin ($\text{K}$)
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** **MANDATORY INDEPENDENT OBSERVATION**.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_rmse_k`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** WMO Guidelines on Verification of Prognostic Weather Analyses.
- **Limitations:** Treats warm surface and cold cloud errors with equal weight unless masked by cloud mask.

### Metric 4: Radiance Flux Conservation (`radiance_flux_conservation_pct`)
- **Classification:** `[DER]` Internal Physical Diagnostic
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{Mass}_{\text{exp}} = (1 - \tau) \sum I_0 + \tau \sum I_1$$
  $$\text{Conservation \%} = \max\left(0.0, 100.0 \times \left(1.0 - \frac{|\sum \hat{I} - \text{Mass}_{\text{exp}}|}{\text{Mass}_{\text{exp}}}\right)\right)$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $\hat{I}, I_0, I_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times C \times H \times W}$, timestep scalar $\tau \in [0, 1]$.
- **Units:** Percentage ($\%$)
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None (measures internal compliance between synthesis and bounding observations).
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_radiance_flux_conservation`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Continuity Equation for Incompressible/Weakly Compressible Atmospheric Layers.
- **Limitations:** Linear mass interpolation is an approximation; rapid non-conservative phase changes (cloud condensation or evaporation) cause physical departures from 100%.

---

### Metric 5: Physical BT Range Compliance (`physical_bt_compliance_pct`)
- **Classification:** `[DER]` Internal Physical Diagnostic
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{Compliance \%} = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum_{(x, y) \in \Omega} \mathbb{I}[180.0\text{ K} \le \hat{T}_{\text{B}}(x, y) \le 330.0\text{ K}] \times 100.0$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $\hat{T}_{\text{B}} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$ in Kelvin.
- **Units:** Percentage ($\%$)
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None (checks against meteorological physical plausibility bounds).
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_physical_bt_compliance`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** ISRO/SAC Radiometric Temperature Bounds for Tropical Troposphere.
- **Limitations:** Verifies that synthesis does not generate unphysical numerical artifacts outside Earth atmospheric temperature envelopes.

### Metric 6: Mean Brightness Temperature Drift (`mean_bt_drift_k`)
- **Classification:** `[DER]` Internal Physical Diagnostic
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{Drift} = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum \hat{T}_{\text{B}} - \left((1 - \tau) \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum T_{\text{B}, 0} + \tau \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum T_{\text{B}, 1}\right)$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $\hat{T}_{\text{B}}, T_{\text{B}, 0}, T_{\text{B}, 1} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$.
- **Units:** Kelvin ($\text{K}$)
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_mean_bt_drift`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Limitations:** Quantifies domain-wide thermal bias introduced by model warping artifacts.

### Metric 7: Fluid Divergence Penalty (`fluid_divergence`)
- **Classification:** `[DER]` Kinetic Regularizer
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{Div} = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \sum \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right)^2$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** Flow field $\vec{u} \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 2 \times H \times W}$ in pixels.
- **Units:** $\text{pixels}^{-2}$
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/physics_eval.py:PhysicsEvaluator.compute_fluid_divergence`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/physics_eval.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Corpetti et al. (2002), *Fluid-Dedicated Optical Flow for Atmospheric Layers*, IEEE TPAMI.
- **Limitations:** Large-scale atmospheric flows exhibit localized convergence/divergence near convective updrafts; this metric monitors numerical instability rather than demanding exact zero divergence.

### Metric 8: Convective Activity Index (`convective_activity_index`)
- **Classification:** `[HEU]` Heuristic Indicator
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{CAI} = \text{clip}\left(10 \cdot \sqrt{|\Delta T_{\text{cool}}| \cdot \max(0, 220 - T_{\min})}, 0.0, 100.0\right)$$
  accompanied by categorical levels:
  - $\text{CAI} < 30$: `NOMINAL`
  - $30 \le \text{CAI} < 60$: `MODERATE`
  - $60 \le \text{CAI} < 85$: `ELEVATED`
  - $\text{CAI} \ge 85$: `SEVERE_CONVECTIVE_UPDRAFT`
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $T_{\text{B}, 0}, T_{\text{B}, 1} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$ (TIR-1 channel in Kelvin).
- **Units:** Dimensionless Index ($0$ to $100$)
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None. **NOT A CALIBRATED PRECIPITATION PROBABILITY.**
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/nowcasting.py:ConvectiveNowcaster.evaluate_convective_risk`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/nowcasting.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Shukla et al. (2017), SAC/ISRO NETRA Algorithm; Roberts & Rutledge (2003), AMS MWR.
- **Limitations:** Measures thermodynamic and morphological updraft signatures at cloud top. Does not guarantee surface rain accumulation without radar/gauge validation.

### Metric 9: Cloud-Top BT Tendency (`cloud_top_bt_tendency_k_15m`)
- **Classification:** `[DER]` Derived Tendency
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\Delta T_{\text{B}} = \min_{(x, y) \in \text{Core}} (T_{\text{B}, 1}(x, y) - T_{\text{B}, 0}(x, y))$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $T_{\text{B}, 0}, T_{\text{B}, 1} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$ (TIR-1).
- **Units:** Kelvin per 15 minutes ($\text{K / 15-min}$) or scaled to scan delta.
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** Direct mathematical difference between two observations.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/nowcasting.py:ConvectiveNowcaster`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/nowcasting.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Mecikalski & Bedka (2006), *Forecasting Convective Initiation by Monitoring the Evolution of Moving Cumulus in Daytime GOES Imagery*, Mon. Wea. Rev.
- **Limitations:** Euler-grid differences can conflate spatial advection of a cloud edge with genuine vertical growth unless Lagrangian object tracking is active.

### Metric 10: Experimental Overshooting-Top Candidates (`experimental_ot_candidates`)
- **Classification:** `[HEU]` Heuristic Indicator / Proxy
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{OT Candidate} \iff (T_{\text{B}} \le \bar{T}_{\text{anvil}} - 4.5\text{ K}) \land (T_{\text{B}} < 210.0\text{ K}) \land \left(\frac{\Delta T_{\text{B}}}{\Delta t} < -2.0\text{ K}\right)$$
  where $\bar{T}_{\text{anvil}}$ is spatial Gaussian smoothed BT ($\sigma = 3.0$ pixels).
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** $T_{\text{B}, 1} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}$.
- **Units:** Integer Count
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None. Labeled as experimental proxy due to absence of NWP sounding data.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/nowcasting.py:ConvectiveNowcaster`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/nowcasting.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Bedka et al. (2010); Proud (2015).
- **Limitations:** Without numerical weather prediction tropopause temperatures ($T_{\text{trop}}$), cirrus anvil undulations or gravity waves can be misidentified as overshooting tops.

### Metric 11: Objective Dvorak CI Number (`dvorak_ci_number`)
- **Classification:** `[HEU]` Heuristic Storm Intensity Index
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$\text{CI} = \text{clip}\left(2.5 + \frac{V_{\text{flow}} - 18.0}{6.0} + \frac{\Delta T_{\text{eye-wall}}}{12.0}, 2.0, 7.5\right)$$
- **Input Arrays & Shapes:** Optical flow magnitude $V_{\text{flow}}$ and radial BT contrast $\Delta T$.
- **Units:** Dvorak Scale (1.0 to 8.0 in 0.5 increments)
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/nowcasting.py:StormTrackPredictor.estimate_dvorak_intensity`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/nowcasting.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Velden et al. (2006), *The Advanced Dvorak Technique (ADT)*, BAMS.
- **Limitations:** Purely satellite-derived kinematic estimate; official operational numbers are published exclusively by IMD Cyclone Warning Division via manual multi-spectral Dvorak analysis.

### Metric 12: Central Pressure & Max Sustained Winds (`central_pressure_hpa`, `max_winds_kmh`)
- **Classification:** `[DER]` Empirical Relationship
- **Mathematical Formula:**
  $$V_{\max}(\text{knots}) = 35.0 + (\text{CI} - 2.5) \cdot 23.0$$
  $$\Delta P = \left(\frac{V_{\max}}{2.3}\right)^{1.0 / 0.76} \quad \implies \quad P_c = 1010.0 - \Delta P \quad [\text{hPa}]$$
- **Units:** $\text{km/h}$ (winds) and $\text{hPa}$ (pressure).
- **Ground Truth Requirement:** None.
- **Implementation Location:** [`src/pipeline/nowcasting.py:StormTrackPredictor`](file:///d:/Projects/BLINK/src/pipeline/nowcasting.py)
- **Authoritative Reference:** Courtney & Knaff (2009), *Adapting the Knaff and Zehr Wind-Pressure Relationship for Operational Use in Tropical Cyclones*, Australian Met. Mag.
- **Limitations:** Standard empirical approximation based on tropical ocean background pressure ($1010\text{ hPa}$).
